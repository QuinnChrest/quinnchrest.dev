A voice assistant for my computer, like "Hey Google" but powered by Claude. Say "Hey Leo" and ask a question: Haiku decides whether Haiku, Sonnet or Opus should answer, and the reply is read out loud. The wake word and speech-to-text run locally, so only the text of the question is sent to Claude.
